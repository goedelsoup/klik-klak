# Actor

An actor is something with a position, a way of moving between positions, and behaviour of
its own. In this corpus an instance distinguishes actors by where their behaviour comes
from — player input or authored script — because that is the axis an authoring vocabulary
has to name. Control can transfer between actors during play, which is why `actor` is a
kind and *player* is a role it plays rather than a class of its own.
