# Walkbox

A walkbox is a region of a room's floor an actor may stand on, carrying the scale the actor
draws at within it. It separates where an actor may *be* from what the room looks like: the
pathable surface is authored independently of the background it sits under, and the depth
scaling that sells perspective is a property of the surface rather than of the art.
